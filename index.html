HOW IT WORKS

Learn at your own pace, one topic at a time

  • Each part includes a short explanation, a video, code examples, and a hands-on worksheet.
  • Every worksheet includes a rubric so you always know what's expected.
  • Topics unlock one at a time — finish one before moving to the next, so nothing gets skipped.
  • Your teacher reviews your submitted work and progress regularly, not just at the end.
